# App data coverage

Touchline uses Football-Data public archives for training and batch results, and The Odds API only for fresh UK match-result and goal-total prices. Collection respects the checked public access rules.

Only E0, SP1, D1, I1 and F1 participate in automatic simulations. Archive prices are indicative because their quote timestamps cannot be verified. Player forecasts, European competition imports and manual CSV uploads are not app features.

Sources can lag or become unavailable. The Engine screen reports failed jobs and reasons for skipped entries. An empty portfolio is not proof that scheduling failed; check its last successful completion and trained leagues.
